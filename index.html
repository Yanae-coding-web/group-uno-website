Proper Disposal

“Clean surroundings, healthy living.”

Why Proper Disposal Matters

Improper waste disposal is one of the biggest challenges in our barangay. When trash is thrown anywhere, it clogs canals, causes flooding, and spreads diseases like dengue and cholera. Plastics that end up in rivers harm fish and marine life, while burning waste releases toxic smoke that affects our lungs.

Proper disposal means segregating waste into categories:

  • Biodegradable: food scraps, garden waste — compost into fertilizer.
  • Recyclable: plastics, bottles, cans, paper — reuse or sell to junk shops.
  • Hazardous: batteries, chemicals, medical waste — special handling required.
  • Residual: items that cannot be recycled — should be minimized.

By practicing segregation, we reduce pollution, save resources, and keep our community healthy. Did you know? 60% of household waste is biodegradable — if composted, it enriches soil instead of filling landfills.
